## What the licence does and does not cover

The **underlying facts** — a breed standard, a tax rate, a national grading
scale — are not copyrightable and you may use them freely regardless of this
licence. What the licence covers is this particular selection, arrangement and
verification of them.

In practice: if you take three numbers out of a CSV, you owe nothing. If you
redistribute the datasets, please credit them.
